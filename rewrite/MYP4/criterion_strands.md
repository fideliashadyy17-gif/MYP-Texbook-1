# MYP Mathematics 4 — Criterion strand lines (Chapters 2–13 + Capstone)

Gap: every MYP4 chapter assessment carries tasks headed `C R I T E R I O N A/B/C/D ·
<name> <minutes> · <marks>` with an *Evidence* line and a rubric, but no strand-mapping
line (MYP1 tasks carry one, e.g. "CRITERIA AND STRANDS A i, ii, iii: …"). The lines below
are ready to insert directly under each task heading. Chapter 1's four lines are already in
`chapter_additions.md`. Modelling tasks are unaffected — their headings already carry the
criterion label ("MODELLING TASK · CRITERION D").

Task-count notes: Chapter 3's assessment has **three** tasks (A, B, D — no Criterion C
task), and Chapter 12's has **three** (A, C, D — no Criterion B task). These are as
designed (their "assessment at a glance" totals say "all three tasks"); no line is added
for a task that does not exist.
